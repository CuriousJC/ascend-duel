# Placeholder art prompt

High-resolution digital illustration of a single question mark made of colored vapor. No text
anywhere in the image other than the question mark itself — no title, no label, no caption, no
signature, no watermark. The question mark and its background, nothing else.

Canvas: portrait, aspect ratio exactly 5:7 (five wide to seven tall). Render as large as you work
natively; around 1060 x 1484 is ideal. It is reduced to 200 x 280 afterwards, and also cropped to
the centered square for a few uses, so anything finer than about a two-hundredth of the width is
lost. Draw for that: one bold, simple form.

Subject: one large question mark, upright and centered, standing about 60% of the canvas height
and sitting entirely inside the centered square of the canvas, with clear space all round so the
square crop keeps the whole mark. It is a thick, rounded, friendly question mark: a full hook
curling over the top, a stem that tapers down, and a separate round dot beneath it. Readable at a
glance as a question mark, even at 64 pixels.

Material: the question mark is made entirely of swirling colored vapor, like a shape drawn in
dense, luminous smoke. The core of each stroke is dense and bright and holds the letterform crisp;
the edges thin into soft wisps that curl a little way off the stroke and fade out, so it reads as
something not yet solid rather than as a carved or painted letter. The wisps never blur the
silhouette. The hook, the stem and the dot all stay clearly separate shapes.

Color: a rainbow running along the stroke, from the tip of the hook down to the dot, in five bands
that blend smoothly into each other — orange, then yellow, then green, then blue, then purple —
so the dot is purple. Use these exact colors. Each band takes its `core` as the stroke's own color,
its `light` at the bright heart of the vapor and its `dark` in the thin outer wisps:

| Band   | dark      | core      | light     |
| ------ | --------- | --------- | --------- |
| orange | `#9E2F13` | `#F06A24` | `#FFB24A` |
| yellow | `#A98200` | `#F2D13D` | `#FFF38A` |
| green  | `#285E35` | `#4E9B57` | `#8BCB78` |
| blue   | `#245A86` | `#4E9ED6` | `#A8DDF4` |
| purple | `#4C2A78` | `#8A5BC7` | `#C8A4F2` |

Equal shares of the stroke for all five, so no one color reads as the color of the whole mark. No
red, no pink, no white rainbow band, no gold.

Shading: smooth and continuous. The vapor glows softly from inside, brightest along the middle of
each stroke. Anti-aliasing, soft gradients and blended edges are all expected and wanted. Do not
posterize, do not dither, do not quantize to a block grid.

Outline: none drawn. The mark is held off the background by its own glow and brightness, which
must stay strong enough to read at card size.

Background: opaque and near-black, around a tenth of full brightness, a neutral charcoal with no
hue of its own so it favours none of the five bands. Essentially flat — no vignette, no second
light source, no scenery, no horizon, no ground plane. Carry a very faint texture across it, a
subtle noise that is barely perceptible and never resolves into a pattern. Never transparent,
never white, never bright. The vapor may bloom very softly onto the background immediately around
the stroke; nothing else casts anything, and there is no cast shadow.

Avoid: a border, a frame, a card shape, a circle or badge behind the mark, a second question mark,
any other letter or number, sparkles, stars, particles, scenery, hands, characters, and clutter of
any kind.

Priority order: readable question mark -> the five bands in order -> vapor material -> fits the
centered square -> subtle background texture.
