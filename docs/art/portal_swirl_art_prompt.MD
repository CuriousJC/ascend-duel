# Portal swirl

You painted the **portal backdrop** for this game: gray fog with two upright rainbow portals, their
shape given by the fog curling round them. This is the piece that sits **on top of the heart of
each of those portals**. The game draws it there and turns it slowly in place, and the player clicks
it to go through. Paint it so that, turning over your painting, it reads as that portal's own swirl
coming alive rather than as a sticker on top of it.

## What it is

One round **swirl of rainbow light**, seen straight on: spiral arms of every hue from red through
violet winding in toward a bright, nearly white eye at the center. It is the same rainbow, the
same softness and the same painterly rendering as the portals you painted, with smooth blended
gradients, soft brushwork and anti-aliasing throughout. Not photographic, not pixel art, not flat
vector, no hard-edged rings, and no concentric target.

## It turns, so it has no up

The game rotates this picture continuously. Everything follows from that:

- **No direction of light.** Unlike the backdrop, nothing is lit from the upper left. There's no
  highlight on one side and no shadow on the other. A lit side would spin round and give the trick
  away.
- **No orientation.** No horizon, no top or bottom, nothing that reads as upright. It must look
  equally right at every angle.
- **The arms are continuous all the way round.** No seam, no break, and no place where one arm stops
  and another starts out of step.
- **Centered exactly.** The eye of the spiral is the exact center of the canvas, because that's the
  point it turns about.

## The edge fades to nothing

- **Fully opaque in the middle**, out to about **55% of the radius**.
- **Fading smoothly to fully transparent** by the edge of the circle, at 100% of the radius. Slow and
  even, like a soft brush, never a visible ring.
- **Fully transparent outside the circle**, including all four corners.
- **No rim, no frame and no fog in it.** Your painting supplies the fog round it; this piece is only
  the light, and its fade is what lets it melt into the rainbow underneath.

## Canvas

- **Exactly 1024x1024, square**, delivered as a **PNG with a real alpha channel**.
- **If you cannot produce transparency**, deliver it on **pure black** (#000000) with the same fade
  done as a fade to black. Never a checkerboard, never magenta, never gray. Pure black can be
  turned back into transparency on our side; nothing else can.
- No text, letters, numbers, signatures, watermarks, logos or user-interface elements.

## Before you hand it back

1. **Rotate it** by a quarter turn, then an eighth. Does it look equally right every time, with no
   lit side and no seam?
2. **Is the eye at the exact center?**
3. **Do the corners and the outer rim fade to fully transparent**, or to pure black, with no ring?
4. **Put it over the heart of one of your portals.** Do the colors belong to that painting?

Name the file `portal-swirl.png` and hand back the bare file.
