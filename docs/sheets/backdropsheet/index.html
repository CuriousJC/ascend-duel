Backdrop sheet

Every room a motif's fights are drawn in front of, once per element it is painted in, with the brief it was painted from: the room's Draw on top and each element's ElementDraw under its picture. A picture not painted yet is shown as the default backdrop the fight really draws, dimmed and outlined in pink. Each picture links to the full file under assets/motifs/.

Tinker Studio

goblins-outer-tinker-studio Goblins · outer chamber · 5 elements

A goblin tinker's studio hall, with nobody in it: a broad floor of scuffed planks and patched flagstones, clear from the foreground to the far wall, under high rafters hung with pulleys, chains and half-finished contraptions. It is a mess. Gears, cogs, springs, bent rivets and loose bolts are strewn across the floor at the edges; workbenches along both side walls are buried in scavenged scrap, disassembled clockwork, lengths of pipe and tools nobody put back. Crates of salvage are stacked unevenly against the walls, and a crooked crane arm leans out over a heap of parts. Nothing matches and everything was something else first.

Bomber Studio

goblins-inner-bomber-studio Goblins · inner chamber · 5 elements

A goblin bomb-maker's studio hall, with nobody in it: a broad floor of scuffed planks and patched flagstones, clear from the foreground to the far wall, under high rafters hung with chains and half-built casings. It is a mess. Bomb parts are scattered everywhere — round iron shells, fuse cord, powder kegs, cracked casings and loose timers — along with nails, shrapnel, broken glass and other sharp scrap heaped in buckets and spilled across the floor at the edges. Masses of unused wire lie in snarled tangles on the workbenches along both side walls and trail down onto the floor. Nothing is stored safely and everything looks like it could go off.

Throne Room

goblins-boss-throne-room Goblins · stairway · 5 elements

A goblin king's throne room, with nobody in it: a broad floor of scuffed planks and patched flagstones, clear from the foreground to the far wall, under high rafters. A bronze throne stands centred against the far wall, dented and patched together from scrap. The two portals stand one on each side of the throne, flanking it. It is still a goblin's hall: heaps of garbage are piled up beside the throne and in the corners and along the walls — broken gears, bent pipe, cracked crates, rags, bones and scavenged junk of every kind.
