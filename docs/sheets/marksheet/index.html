Mark sheet

Every form mark and every cost tick, at the sizes the game draws them and blown up 6x with nearest-neighbor. Read the actual-size table first — there is no zoom anywhere in the game, and reviewing only the enlarged one is how a mark comes to look acceptable in review and clunky in play. Under each cell: the ink's drawn size, how many pixels of it are opaque, and what share of those are near-black. That last figure is the contour, and a 0% is a mark with nothing holding it against a pale card.
